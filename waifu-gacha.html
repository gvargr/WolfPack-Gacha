What is this?
A randomised anime character generator that builds a complete Stable Diffusion prompt and displays results as a holographic collector card. Each pull produces a unique character with a matching background scene and rarity-scaled quality tags.

Age Policy
All generated characters are designed and intended to be 18 years of age or older. The physique, naming, and prompt tags are built with adult characters in mind. NSFW mode is for adult content between adult characters only.

How to use
1. Toggle SFW / NSFW in the header to set your content level.
2. Click PULL to generate a random character.
3. Re-roll as many times as you like -- each pull is free.
4. Click COPY PROMPT and paste into SwarmUI, A1111, ComfyUI or Forge.
5. Click COPY NEG and paste into the negative prompt field.
6. Click SAVE CARD to download the card as a PNG image.

Rarity
Rarity affects both the card visuals and the prompt quality tags.
Common → clean result  |  Rare → cinematic lighting  |  Legendary → masterpiece quality, god rays, epic framing and an ornate gilded collector border -- all baked directly into the prompt.

Background & Scene
The background is weighted by race and refined by outfit. An Elf in Fantasy Robes gets a moonlit elven grove with floating scrolls. A Vampire in a Gothic outfit gets a candlelit dungeon. The scene tag is visible on the card and included in the prompt.

NSFW & Futanari
NSFW mode unlocks a wider outfit pool with explicit tags and updates the negative prompt automatically. On Waifu pulls there is a 15% chance of a Futanari roll -- this boosts the rarity one tier and adds a FUTA badge to the card.

Save Card
Downloads a PNG of the collector card with all stats, rarity effects, corner gems and star strip. Works fully offline -- no internet connection required.
